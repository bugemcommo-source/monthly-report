**This is a deferral, not a cancellation, and not a request of Mancom.** The purchase was
already approved; the department chose to hold off buying until CLIMBS' feedback is in,
because it may change where the power station is bought from. The report should say so
plainly rather than showing the item as slipped.

**What it is for, confirmed by the owner on 16 September 2026: backup power at the
Ownership Meeting in October.** That closes the question left open in July about what the
power station would keep running.

**CLIMBS** is the insurance cooperative BUGEMCO works with. **Re-Coop** is a CLIMBS project,
and the department is waiting on their feedback about a **renewable power source** under it.

**This is not what the September CLIMBS meeting is about.** That meeting is about API
integration between the CLIMBS and CAC systems — see `../plans/2026-09.md`. The two were
recorded as one item in error on 15 September and separated the next day.

Still to confirm, whenever convenient: what Re-Coop covers, and whether a renewable source
would replace the power station or sit alongside it.
