## July 2026 in detail

From the owner's July intake.

**Regular, every week**
- **Mancom Meeting** — the management committee's weekly meeting.
- **Policy Review** — the weekly session that works through policies.

**With outside parties**
- **SCL Dura** — the supplier of the Queuing System.
- **MASS-SPECC** — on support tickets. MASS-SPECC is the federation BUGEMCO belongs to.
  *(Which platform the tickets are raised against is deliberately not named here. This
  file is published. Naming a core system on a public page, in a month when its backup is
  reported as unavailable, is targeting information rather than a status update.)*
- **ACCESS branding auditors** — an audit against the ACCESS branding standard.
  **ACCESS** stands for **A Competitive Choice for Excellence in Service and Soundness**
  (confirmed by the owner, July 2026 intake). In plain terms it is a standard a
  cooperative is measured against, covering how well it serves members and how sound it
  is as an organisation. Carrying the brand tells members the coop has been checked
  against it.

  **The audit produced work for ICT.** Three areas were raised, confirmed by the owner in
  the July 2026 intake:

  1. **Website improvement** — work on the coop's public website.
  2. **Support integration** — bringing support together rather than running it in
     separate pieces.
  3. **Infrastructure improvement** — the equipment and networks underneath everything
     else.

  *Still to confirm with the owner:* what specifically was asked for under each heading,
  whether any deadline was set, and whether these are formal findings the coop must answer
  or recommendations it may choose to act on. Those are different things and the report
  should not blur them.

**Inside the coop**
- **Department Meeting** — the ICT department's own meeting, held during the month.
- **System owners** — meetings with the departments that own each system, to present the
  in-house systems the department has built.

Note on the weekly meetings: they were previously only written down when something
notable came out of them, which is why earlier months look emptier than they were. From
July they are recorded as attended every month.
