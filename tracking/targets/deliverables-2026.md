## A few terms in plain words

- **Vulnerability assessment** — a check of our systems for weaknesses an attacker
  could use, done once a year.
- **User access review** — going through who can get into which system and removing
  anyone who no longer needs it.
- **Secure development standards** — the agreed way we write and release software so
  mistakes are caught before they reach members.
- **Data Privacy Act** — the Philippine law on how personal information must be handled.
