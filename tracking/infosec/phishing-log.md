# Phishing Simulation Results

We send staff a harmless fake phishing email to see who would fall for a real one.
Nobody is punished for clicking — the point is to find out where more training is
needed.

## What each column means

- **Emails sent** — how many staff were sent the test email.
- **Opened** — how many opened it.
- **Clicked the link** — how many clicked the fake link inside it.
- **Gave details** — how many went further and typed in a username or password on
  the fake page. This is the most serious outcome.
- **Reported it** — how many spotted it and reported it to ICT. Higher is better,
  and this is the number we most want to grow.

## What "click rate" means

Out of everyone who received the test email, the share who clicked the fake link.
**Lower is better.** The department target is **1% or less**.

Always state it in plain words in the report — for example
"*N* of *M* staff clicked the test email (*X*%)" — so nobody has to guess whether
the percentage means passing or failing.

The letters are deliberate. An example with real-looking numbers sitting directly
above an empty table is too easily mistaken for the actual result.

There is an open question about whether the target recorded elsewhere is a failure
rate or a pass rate. See the note at the end of `../targets/bsc-annual-2026.md`.
