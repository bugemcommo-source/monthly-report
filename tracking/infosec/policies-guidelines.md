## Digital agreements — drafted August 2026

BSC target for 2026: **5 digital agreements standardised.**

**All five were drafted on 20 August 2026.** They are held in the department's own
documentation repository, which is kept outside this folder and is never published. The owner pointed to it in the August intake.

| # | Agreement | Who signs it, and when | Versions |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Terms of Use for BUGEMCO Digital Services** | Anyone, before they use a BUGEMCO digital service | 2 — member, non-member customer |
| 2 | **Account and Credential Responsibility Undertaking** | Anyone who is given a login | 1 |
| 3 | **Privacy Notice and Acknowledgment** | The person the information is about — or the adult acting for a child | 4 — member, non-member customer, employee or applicant, parent or guardian |
| 4 | **Consent for Optional Processing** | Only people who choose to opt in | 1 |
| 5 | **Parental Permission for a Child's Data** | A parent or guardian, at Learning Center enrolment | 1 |

**Five agreements, eight documents.** The Privacy Notice is written four times because it
has to speak to four different audiences, which is why the folder appears to hold more than
five files. Anyone counting the files will get eight; the agreement count is five.

**Status: drafted, not yet issued.** None of the five has been given to a member yet. Each
one carries conditions that have to be met first — chiefly that no blank may remain in any
part the signer reads. Some of those blanks are owed by other departments, not by ICT.

**Decision recorded 26 August 2026 — English only.** All five had been drafted on the
condition that an approved Cebuano/Bisaya version existed before issue. That condition was
put to the Supervisor-Programmer, Software Development and Information Security, and the
decision was to proceed in **English only**. It is recorded here because it was a decision
about all five at once.

**They are waiting to be presented to Mancom.** Confirmed by the owner in the August
intake. That presentation is the next step for all five at once.

**Reopened by the owner on 16 September 2026 and settled the other way.** The August
intake had counted these as standardised at **drafted**, making the target 5 of 5. The
owner's own objection — they have not been presented or approved — carried, so **nothing
is claimed against the target** while the agreements sit inside the department.

`Digital agreements standardised` is recorded as **a dash, with five drafted noted beside
it**. The drafting is reported as work delivered, under Information Security, not as a
target reached. See `../targets/bsc-annual-2026.md`.
