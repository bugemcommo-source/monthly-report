## Screens on record (August 2026)

Two screenshots supplied by the owner, now in the July report:
`reports/2026-07-july/img/cms-login.jpg` and `cms-dashboard.jpg`.

**Read off the screens, not confirmed by the owner** — this answers part of "what it
covers", but the owner has not yet confirmed any of it:

- It scores loan applications against **eight factors** — capacity to pay, character,
  capital, collateral LTV, condition, climate, marketability and mode of payment — out
  of 115, with a decision band (the sample shows 88, and "≥ 85 approves") and two
  mandatory gates that can hold a file regardless of total score.
- It runs across **7 branches** and describes itself as audit-ready.
- It carries a full decision workflow: draft, branch pool, under investigation, returned
  to officer, manager review, credit committee review, approved / approved with
  conditions / declined / on hold, for posting, posted, cancelled.
- It records the **tool's recommendation separately from the decision made**, so a
  manager can see where staff agreed with it and where they did not. That is the
  interesting thing about this system and it is worth saying at the presentation.
- It handles CIBI transactions and exports to CSV.
- The figures on screen are **test data** (17 transactions). The report says so.
