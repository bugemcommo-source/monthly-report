## Monthly log
- 2026-09 — **What RMS does, from the owner's walkthrough "Checking a Voucher" (supplied 8 October 2026, 8 screenshots):** staff upload scanned vouchers (journal vouchers and check vouchers); the system reads each scan by machine (date, check number, payee, description, amount, account lines) and puts it in a "Waiting to be checked" queue; a checker compares the fields with the scan, corrects anything wrong, and files it, or sends it back. It flags fields where two readings of the scan differ. Sections seen: Overview, Upload Record, Document Library, Analytics. **The screenshots show real vouchers** (payee names, amounts, check numbers, the coop's TIN, staff names) — all 8 originals are held in `private/` only. **Owner's ruling, 8 October 2026 ("a"): publish only the checking-queue screen, with voucher codes, filing details (who and when) and amounts blurred** — `intake/2026-09/rms-checking-queue.png`.
