What is switched off, and why it matters

Two things stopped when the subscription ended:

  • The short lessons that teach staff how to spot a scam
  • The harmless fake scam emails that test whether the lessons stuck

Staff are the most common way an attacker gets in. Training and testing are what keep that door closed, and both are paused.

This is not a shortfall in effort. The department chose to look at what is available before committing to another multi-year term.

While it is paused

The phishing target cannot be measured. Not missed — not measurable.

Read per person, not per total. KnowBe4 costs about 1.9 times as much per person per year as Wizer — roughly 93% more. That is the like-for-like comparison.

The yearly bill rises much further than that, because the number of people covered rises too: from 29 to 100. Php 50,000 a year becomes Php 333,333 a year — about 6.7 times the annual spend. Most of that increase buys coverage the coop did not have, not a dearer product.

Two things make this less than a clean comparison, and both should be settled before the figures are used to decide anything. The KnowBe4 quote covers 100 accounts — if the coop needs 29, or 60, the price should be re-quoted for the real number. And it is a three-year commitment against a one-year one, which is a different kind of decision even at the same price.
