RMS — Record Management System

1st presentation held in September

What it does with a voucher:

  • Staff upload scanned vouchers — journal vouchers and check vouchers
  • The system reads each scan by machine: date, check number, payee, description, amount and account lines
  • It puts each one in a “Waiting to be checked” queue, and flags any field where two readings of the scan differ
  • A checker compares the fields with the scan, corrects anything wrong, and files it — or sends it back

Still to confirm: which department will use it.
